# How to Get to Clip & Chill: Directions, Parking and Transit

Clip & Chill Barbershop is at **4099 Erin Mills Pkwy #4, Mississauga, ON L5L 3P9** — in the plaza just south of Folkway Drive, roughly two minutes from Erin Mills Town Centre.

This page covers exactly how to get here, where to park, and how long it takes from around the city. If you'd rather just call, we're at **(905) 606-2212**.

## Finding the Unit

The address ends in **#4**, and that matters — the shop is a unit within a plaza rather than a standalone building. Once you're in the lot, look for the Clip & Chill signage on the storefront.

The plaza fronts directly onto Erin Mills Parkway, so if you're driving south from Folkway Drive it's on your right shortly after the intersection. Coming north, it's on your left.

If you overshoot, the Erin Mills Town Centre area is immediately north — turning around is easy in either direction.

**There's a free parking lot directly in front of the shop.** No meters, no time limits, no permits, and no paid app to download.

Accessible parking is available in the lot.

## Driving Routes

**From the north (Streetsville, Meadowvale, Brampton):** come south on Erin Mills Parkway. The shop is on the right, just past Folkway Drive.

**From the south (Port Credit, Clarkson, Lorne Park):** head north on Erin Mills Parkway. The plaza is on the left after you pass the Erin Mills Town Centre area.

**From the east (Square One, Cooksville, Hurontario, Etobicoke):** the 403 westbound is usually the fastest route, exiting at Erin Mills Parkway and heading south.

**From the west (Oakville, Milton):** the 403 eastbound to the Erin Mills Parkway exit, then south.
